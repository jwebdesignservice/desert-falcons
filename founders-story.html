01

A Child's Dream

Summer 1982, Al-Baha Province

I was ten years old. We didn't have ready-made toys.

So I made my own.

"Wood from the house. Rusty nails from my grandfather's workshop. A small car. It wasn't beautiful, but it was mine."

I pulled it through dusty alleys, making engine sounds with my mouth. I didn't know it then, but I was planting a seed.

Chapter 04

I was telling Reema about my dream. I expected encouragement. A proud smile. Instead, she said:

"
Saif – the son that you don't have.
"

The words hit like an arrow. I left the room. Sat alone in the dark.

But that night, I realized something powerful:

I don't need a son to build a legacy.

My daughters ARE my legacy.

That word became the fuel for SAIF.
