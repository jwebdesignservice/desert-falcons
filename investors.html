FAQ

Frequently Asked

Answers to the questions serious investors ask.

When will you actually accept investments?

Once we have finalised our legal structure and are fully CMA-compliant. All registered investors will be notified directly before any public announcement.

What is the minimum investment?

SAR 50,000. This threshold ensures we work exclusively with serious partners who share the long-term vision.

Can I invest if I'm outside Saudi Arabia?

This will be determined based on future regulatory structures. Register your interest now and we will keep you informed as the framework develops.

What do investors receive in return?

Full details will be provided in official offering documents when available. Anticipated structure includes information rights, pro-rata rights, and tag-along rights with a 1× non-participating liquidation preference.

What regulations will apply to future investments?

Any future offering will comply with the Capital Market Authority's Rules on the Offer of Securities and Continuing Obligations. Depending on instrument type, SAMA regulations may also apply.

Is Desert Falcons Collective licensed to accept investments?

No. We are currently in the community-building phase and do not accept investments. Any future fundraising will be conducted only after obtaining all necessary regulatory approvals from the CMA.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We employ industry-standard security measures and handle your personal data in accordance with Saudi laws and regulations. All information provided will be kept strictly confidential and used only for the purpose of evaluating potential investment opportunities.
