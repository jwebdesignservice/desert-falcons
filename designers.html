01 The Invitation

Where SAIF Finds Its Soul

Design is where SAIF finds its soul. We are creating a new design language – one rooted in the sword and palm, inspired by the vastness of our desert, the warmth of our hospitality, and the pride of our identity.

This is not about copying European luxury. It is about expressing who we are through every curve, every surface, every material.

We are looking for passionate Saudi designers who believe in the vision and want to be part of history – contributing their creativity, building their portfolios, and shaping the first Saudi luxury car.
