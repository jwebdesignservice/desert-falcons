A Problem Worth Solving

We are building the first Saudi luxury vehicle from the ground up – and we need the finest engineering minds in the Kingdom to make it happen.

Every system must be engineered to the highest global standards while enduring the unique demands of our climate and roads.

This is not an assembly line job. This is a chance to design, innovate, and problem-solve at the frontier of Saudi automotive engineering.
